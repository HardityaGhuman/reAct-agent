# Company Policies for X

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 30 PTO days per year. Unused PTO does not roll over.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Working Hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

## Annual Raise
All employees receive a 3% annual raise. Employees earning under $60,000 receive 5% instead. A performance rating of "exceeds expectations" adds 2 percentage points. Raises are capped at $10,000.
